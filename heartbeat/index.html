Heartbeat

By Frank M. Taylor

Six

Too bright!

Colors are everywhere: Dark browns, yellows, blues, and grays.

They flash and undulate from one to the next. They shine and dim from every possible direction. The way the colors dart from the ceiling to walls, floor, and then back to the top is dizzying.

This is a kind of dizziness that makes the ears itch.

Colors are scratching my ears!

Five

Too loud!

Artificial tones of strange pitches resonate deep into the sternum, travel up to the shoulder, into the neck and then out through the eyes. A loudness that’s shaking bones and flesh together.

The sounds shake everything from the bladder to the toes. Everything is vibrating at different-but-still-awful speeds — including the eyes.

The noises are hurting my eyes!

Four

Too stinky!

In a room that smells of old metal and molds, the familiar scents of gunpowder drift away.

Disgusting odors carrying hints of barbecue fill the room through the door. One particular smell is rotten eggs with a patina of burning. That one is settling around the eyes, nostrils, and getting into the lungs.

Another smell, reminiscent of old cat urine in shag carpet, is swirling in the bottom air. That one is warmer than the other airs. That one pours in under the door and fills up the space.

These smells are making it hard to think!

Three

Too much danger!

The smell of wet copper is mixing with the smells of barbecue, mold, and…. Not wet copper — wet coppers. They’re different. One was sick. One was drinking. One is…

Sammy, mutters the man.

Awake!

Sammy, come here.

The man pushes himself up to survey the old concrete room.

He sees what I’ve been trying to tell him.

His eyes go from the dead man next to him to the dead one at the computer. Then he sees the giant metal tube in the room next door. He sees it’s burning. He looks around and listens to the roaring words.

He understands.

Sammy, we didn’t stop the launch. But, we still can keep it from getting airborne.

He points at a side of the computer console with a large switch.

Two

…much love.

This man found me, taught me, fed me, loved me. He taught his pups to love me.

I know how his heart beats for them. And when he looks at me, I hear how it beats for me.

Sammy, pull that switch down.

I jump to the computer console. I bite on the switch, like I did the man who hurt him, pulling down.

One

Heartbeat

Good girl, Sammy.

He needs me.

The air in the room fills with intolerable heat as the missile fails to penetrate a closed bay door.

He runs his hands through my burning fur. Everything hurts; I try not to whimper.

His heart beats for me. Mine beats for him.

He whispers the phrase I love; my favorite. The last words are different.

I'll be your shield, and you'll be… the world's…
